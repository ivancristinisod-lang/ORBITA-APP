# ORBITA CANON — V0.1

Status: **DRAFT / HQ FOUNDATION**

## Definition

ORBITA is an **agent of relational intelligence**.

It is not defined as a CRM, social network, contact manager or mass outreach tool.

## Initial wedge

Professional networking for:

- founders;
- entrepreneurs;
- operators around startup ecosystems.

Long-term scope may expand beyond professional relationships.

## Core product principle

> ORBITA does not maximize contacts. It maximizes valuable relationships.

## Unit of intelligence

ORBITA reasons about the relationship, not only the person.

Conceptually:

```text
Person
+ Context
+ History
+ Intent
+ Reciprocity
+ Time
= Relationship
```

## Initial capability families

- Memory
- Radar
- Next best relational action
- Meeting Brief
- Connect / introductions

## UX direction

ORBITA should help answer:

- Who should I talk to?
- What am I forgetting?
- What changed in my network?
- What did I promise?
- What context matters before this meeting?
- Who should know each other?
- Which relationship needs attention?
- Why is ORBITA recommending this?

## Trust principles

- Do not fabricate relational facts.
- Separate fact from inference.
- Preserve evidence.
- Make correction possible.
- Avoid manipulative scoring of human relationships.
- Do not automate authenticity away.
- Treat permissions and privacy as product features.

## Current technical decision

The Visual MVP is frontend-only.

No canonical decision has yet been made for:

- database provider;
- vector database;
- graph database;
- LLM provider;
- agent framework;
- auth provider;
- integrations architecture.

Those decisions require product and data specifications first.
